## 💡 **Tips for Best Results**

### Good Questions ✅
- Specific product/standard names: "IS 10500 requirements"
- Clear intent: "What are the safety requirements for..."
- Technical terms: "compressive strength", "luminous efficacy"
- Testing focus: "Which tests are required for..."

### Avoid ❌
- Very vague: "Tell me about standards"
- Too broad: "Everything about construction"
- Non-BIS topics: "How to build a house?"
- Personal advice: "Should I buy this product?"
